Auction Battle Privacy Policy

App: Auction Battle (package com.huanghonsen.auctionbattle) · Effective date: 28 September 2026

Summary

Auction Battle is an online multiplayer game. It uses the data described below only to run matches and, if you create one, your player account. We do not sell or share this data with third parties. Your nickname and match activity are shown to the other players in your room. An account is optional: you can play as a guest.

Online matches

While you play, the app connects over the internet to our game server (Tencent Cloud). To run a match it sends:

  • your nickname (a random name by default; do not use your real name);
  • a random reconnect code issued by our game server and saved on your device, so you can return to your seat after a dropped connection. It is not linked to your Google account or any device identifier;
  • your in-game actions (soul choice, bids, wagers, ready taps).

Like any internet connection, the server necessarily sees your IP address while you are connected. It uses the address only in memory, to slow down repeated wrong passwords, and does not store it. The game server does not write IP addresses, nicknames, usernames or codes to its logs; its logs contain only room codes, times and aggregate counts, and rotated game logs older than 14 days are cleaned up when the server runner starts a game-server process. Match state is held in memory only while the room is open and is discarded at the latest 10 minutes after the match ends. The cloud provider may keep standard network records under its own policies.

Player account (optional)

If you register, our game server stores:

  • your username and nickname, and the date the account was created;
  • values derived from your password (a random salt and two one-way keys). Your password itself never leaves your phone: the app proves you know it without sending it, and the server cannot recover it;
  • hashed sign-in tokens for up to 5 devices, each valid for 60 days;
  • your game statistics: matches played, wins, top-three finishes, best rank, total points and how often you used each soul.

This lets you keep your nickname and statistics and continue a match on another phone. Other players see only your nickname. The account file is kept on the game server with dated daily copies for 7 days as a backup. There is no e-mail or phone number on the account, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered.

Chat, friends, reports and the leaderboard

  • Chat. Room chat and messages to friends are passed by the server to the players they are for and are not stored on the server. Your phone keeps them only until the app closes. Offensive words, links and phone numbers are masked automatically. You can turn room chat off in Settings.
  • Friends (accounts only). Your friend list, waiting friend requests and the players you blocked are stored with your account. Friends see your nickname, tier and whether you are online or in a game.
  • Blocking. A blocked player's messages, invitations and friend requests no longer reach you. Guests' block lists are kept only on the phone.
  • Reports. When you report a player, the server stores which player was reported (their account, or a temporary guest id), a random id of the reporter, the reason and at most 60 characters of the reported message, for up to 30 days. After several different players report the same player, that player's chat is paused for a while and a reported nickname is replaced by a plain one. Reports are only used for this.
  • Ranked points and the leaderboard. Quick matches played while signed in add or remove ranked points, stored with your account's statistics. The leaderboard shows the nickname, tier and points of the top 100 players to everyone.

Community rules

Nicknames and chat must not contain insults, harassment, hate, sexual content, advertising or spam, other people's personal information, or pretend to be the game's staff. Nicknames that break these rules are refused; players reported by several others are muted and renamed as described above, and repeated breaches lead to longer mutes. Use Report and Block on any chat line or player.

Deleting your account

In the app, open My account → Delete account and confirm with your password. The account, its statistics, friends, block list, ranked points, reports about it and its sign-in tokens are removed from the server immediately; the daily backup copies that still contain it are overwritten within 7 days. If you can no longer open the app, see how to request deletion.

What the app does not do

  • It uses only two Android permissions: internet access (for matches) and vibration (for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). It does not use location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage outside the app.
  • It has no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services and no in-app purchases.
  • Apart from the data described above, it does not send any information about you or your device off your device.

Reconnect session mappings are held in server memory and can remain after a room closes, until the server process restarts. The current multiplayer connection uses unencrypted WebSocket transport; passwords are never sent over it, but do not use sensitive information as your nickname or username.

Data kept on your device

Your settings (language, volume, graphics, font size, vibration), nickname, reconnect code and sign-in token are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. The app opts out of Android cloud backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them; it does not delete a server account (see above).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us using the channel below and we will delete it.

Changes

If a future version starts to collect or share other data, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change.

拍卖大作战 隐私政策

应用：拍卖大作战（包名 com.huanghonsen.auctionbattle）· 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 28 日

摘要

拍卖大作战是联机多人游戏，只把下述数据用于进行对局，以及（如果你注册）你的玩家账号。我们不出售、也不向第三方共享这些数据。同一房间的其他玩家会看到你的昵称及对局信息。账号是可选的，你可以用访客身份游玩。

联机对局

游玩时，应用会通过互联网连接我们的游戏服务器（腾讯云）。为进行对局，会发送：

  • 你的昵称（默认是随机名字，请勿使用真实姓名）；
  • 服务器随机生成并保存在你设备上的重连码，用于断线后回到原座位；它不关联你的 Google 账号或任何设备标识；
  • 你在游戏中的操作（选择灵魂、出价、下注、准备）。

与所有网络连接一样，连接期间服务器必然会看到你的 IP 地址；服务器只在内存中用它限制反复输错密码，不会保存。游戏服务器不会把 IP 地址、昵称、账号名或任何代码写入日志；日志只包含房间号、时间和汇总统计，服务器运行脚本在启动游戏服务进程时清理超过 14 天的轮换日志。对局状态只在房间开放期间保存在内存中，最迟在对局结束 10 分钟后丢弃。云服务商可能依其自身政策保留标准的网络记录。

玩家账号（可选）

注册后，游戏服务器会保存：

  • 你的账号名、昵称和注册日期；
  • 由密码推导出的数值（随机盐值和两个单向密钥）。密码本身从不离开你的手机：应用在不发送密码的情况下证明你知道它，服务器也无法还原密码；
  • 最多 5 台设备的登录凭证（只保存哈希值），各自 60 天有效；
  • 你的战绩：对局数、胜场、前三名次数、最佳名次、累计积分，以及各灵魂的使用次数。

这让你保留昵称与战绩，并能换一台手机继续对局。其他玩家只能看到你的昵称。账号文件保存在游戏服务器上，另有每日一份、保留 7 天的备份。账号不含邮箱或手机号，因此忘记密码无法找回。

聊天、好友、举报与排行榜

  • 聊天。房间聊天和好友私聊由服务器转发给对应玩家，服务器不保存；手机上的记录在关闭应用后即消失。不当词语、链接和电话号码会被自动遮挡。可在设置中关闭房间聊天。
  • 好友（仅限账号）。好友列表、待处理的好友申请和你屏蔽的玩家随账号保存。好友可以看到你的昵称、段位以及是否在线或正在对局。
  • 屏蔽。被屏蔽的玩家的消息、邀请和好友申请不会再送达你。访客的屏蔽名单只保存在手机上。
  • 举报。举报玩家时，服务器会保存被举报的玩家（其账号，或临时的访客编号）、举报人的随机编号、举报原因以及最多 60 个字的被举报消息，最长 30 天。同一玩家被多位不同玩家举报后，会被暂停发言一段时间，被举报的昵称会改为普通名字。举报只用于此目的。
  • 段位与排行榜。登录后进行的快速匹配会增减段位分，随账号战绩保存。排行榜向所有玩家显示前 100 名的昵称、段位和分数。

社区规则

昵称和聊天不得包含辱骂、骚扰、仇恨、色情、广告或刷屏、他人的个人信息，也不得冒充游戏官方人员。违反规则的昵称无法使用；被多位玩家举报的玩家会按上述方式被禁言并改名，屡次违规禁言时间更长。每条聊天和每位玩家都可以举报和屏蔽。

删除账号

在应用中打开我的账号 → 删除账号并输入密码确认。账号、战绩、好友、屏蔽名单、段位分、针对该账号的举报与登录凭证会立即从服务器删除；仍包含它的每日备份会在 7 天内被覆盖。如果已无法打开应用，请见申请删除账号。

本应用不会做的事

  • 只使用两项 Android 权限：网络访问（用于对局）和振动（可在设置中关闭的触感反馈）；不使用位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录以及应用以外的存储。
  • 没有广告，没有统计分析或崩溃上报服务，没有应用内购买。
  • 除上述数据外，不会把任何关于你或你设备的信息发送到设备之外。

重连会话映射保存在服务器内存中，房间关闭后仍可能保留，直到服务器进程重启。当前联机连接使用未加密的 WebSocket 传输；密码从不经由它发送，但请勿把敏感信息用作昵称或账号名。

保存在你设备上的数据

设置（语言、音量、画质、字号、振动）、昵称、重连码与登录凭证只保存在本应用于你设备上的私有存储中，并且本应用关闭了 Android 云备份。卸载应用或清除应用数据即会删除这些数据，但不会删除服务器上的账号（见上文）。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。如果你认为儿童通过游戏提供了个人信息，请通过下方渠道联系我们，我们会删除。

变更

如果未来的版本开始收集或共享其他数据，本政策会在该版本发布前更新，并修改上方的生效日期。
