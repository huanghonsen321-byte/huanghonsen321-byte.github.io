Auction Battle Privacy Policy

App: Auction Battle (package com.huanghonsen.auctionbattle) · Effective date: 26 September 2026

Summary

Auction Battle does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The game works fully offline.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use any Android permission, including internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage outside the app.
  • It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services and no in-app purchases.
  • It does not send any information about you or your device off your device.

Data kept on your device

Your campaign progress and settings (language, volume, graphics, font size) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device so that you can continue later. They are never uploaded, and the app opts out of Android cloud backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version starts to collect or share any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: GitHub Issues

拍卖大作战 隐私政策

应用：拍卖大作战（包名 com.huanghonsen.auctionbattle）· 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 26 日

摘要

拍卖大作战不收集任何个人数据，不在任何服务器上存储、共享或出售任何个人数据。游戏可完全离线运行。

本应用不会做的事

  • 不申请也不使用任何 Android 权限，包括网络、位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录以及应用以外的存储。
  • 没有账号与登录，没有广告，没有统计分析或崩溃上报服务，没有应用内购买。
  • 不会把任何关于你或你设备的信息发送到设备之外。

保存在你设备上的数据

征程进度与设置（语言、音量、画质、字号）只保存在本应用于你设备上的私有存储中，以便下次继续。它们从不上传，并且本应用关闭了 Android 云备份。卸载应用或清除应用数据即会删除这些数据。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的信息。

变更

如果未来的版本开始收集或共享任何数据，本政策会在该版本发布前更新，并修改上方的生效日期。
