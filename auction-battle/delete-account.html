Auction Battle: delete your account

Developer: huanghonsen321-byte · App: Auction Battle (com.huanghonsen.auctionbattle)

In the app (immediate)

  1. On the home screen, tap My account.
  2. Tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Without the app

Open an issue at GitHub Issues titled "Delete Auction Battle account" and give your username (never your password). Because accounts have no e-mail, we will ask you to confirm ownership, for example by changing your nickname in the app to a word we give you. The account is deleted within 7 days of confirmation.

What is deleted

  • Username, nickname and creation date.
  • The password-derived keys and all sign-in tokens.
  • All game statistics (matches, wins, ranks, points, soul use).

Deletion from the live account file is immediate. The server's dated daily backup copies are kept for 7 days and then overwritten, so the account disappears from them within 7 days. No account data is kept after that. Match data is never stored beyond 10 minutes after a match ends.

拍卖大作战：删除账号

在应用内（立即生效）

  1. 在首页点我的账号。
  2. 点删除账号，输入密码并确认。

无法使用应用时

在 GitHub Issues 新建标题为“删除拍卖大作战账号”的问题，写明你的账号名（切勿填写密码）。由于账号没有邮箱，我们会请你确认所有权，例如在应用中把昵称改成我们指定的词。确认后 7 天内删除。

删除的内容

  • 账号名、昵称与注册日期。
  • 由密码推导出的密钥及所有登录凭证。
  • 全部战绩（对局数、胜场、名次、积分、灵魂使用次数）。

当前账号文件中的数据立即删除；服务器每日备份保留 7 天后覆盖，因此 7 天内账号会从备份中消失，之后不保留任何账号数据。对局数据在对局结束 10 分钟后即不再保存。
